title: Hostel Rules
category: hostel
audience: student
effective: 2024-01-01

Hostel Residence
All first-year undergraduate students must reside in the hostel. Rooms are allotted by the Hostel Office at the start of each academic year. Residents must follow the institute and hostel regulations at all times.

Visitors
Visitors are allowed between 10:00 and 18:00 in the designated visitor lounge only. Every visitor must register at the hostel gate and show a photo ID. Overnight stay of visitors is not permitted under any circumstances.

Leave and Late Entry
A resident who plans to stay away from campus overnight must submit a leave application on the hostel portal at least 24 hours in advance. The hostel gate closes at 22:30 for undergraduate residents. Late entry after 22:30 needs prior permission from the warden.

Room Change
Room change requests are accepted only during the first two weeks of each semester. Requests go to the warden and are approved subject to availability.

Mess Rebate
A resident absent from the mess for more than 5 consecutive days may claim a rebate on the mess fee if the Mess Office receives written notice before departure.

Prohibited Items
Electric heaters, induction cooktops and other high-power appliances are not allowed in hostel rooms. Security staff may inspect rooms and confiscate prohibited items.
