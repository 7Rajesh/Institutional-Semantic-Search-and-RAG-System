title: Research and PhD Information
category: research
audience: student
effective: 2023-06-01

PhD Duration
A full-time PhD candidate must complete the programme in a minimum of 3 years and a maximum of 6 years from the date of joining. Part-time candidates may take up to 8 years.

PhD Coursework
Every PhD candidate must earn at least 12 credits of coursework in the first year. The doctoral committee may prescribe additional courses.

Comprehensive Examination
The comprehensive examination must be completed within 18 months of joining. A candidate who fails may reappear once within 6 months.

Thesis Submission
A candidate may submit the thesis after at least one paper is accepted in a peer-reviewed journal. The thesis must show a similarity index below 10% in the plagiarism check.

Seed Grants and Laboratory Access
New faculty members may apply for a seed grant of up to Rs 5,00,000. Shared laboratories are booked through the research portal at least 3 days in advance.

Research Ethics
Research involving human participants requires approval from the Institutional Ethics Committee before data collection begins.
