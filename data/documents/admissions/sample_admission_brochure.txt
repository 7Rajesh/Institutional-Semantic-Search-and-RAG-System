Eligibility for Undergraduate Admission
Applicants must have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and secured at least 75% aggregate marks. The requirement is 65% for candidates from reserved categories and persons with disabilities. Admission is through the national entrance examination and the counselling process.

Fee Structure
The tuition fee is Rs 1,25,000 per semester. The hostel fee is Rs 20,000 per semester. A one-time refundable caution deposit of Rs 10,000 is collected at admission.

Fee Payment and Refund
Semester fees are due within 10 days of the start of the semester. A late fine of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date. A candidate who withdraws before classes begin receives a refund of 90% of the fees paid.

Scholarships
The merit-cum-means scholarship gives a full tuition waiver to students whose annual family income is below Rs 1,00,000. Students renew the scholarship every year by submitting an income certificate and keeping a CGPA of at least 7.0.

Document Verification
Candidates must bring original mark sheets, the category certificate if applicable, and a government photo ID to document verification on the reporting day.
