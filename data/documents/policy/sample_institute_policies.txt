Anti-Ragging Policy
The institute follows a zero-tolerance policy on ragging. Any student may report an incident to the Anti-Ragging Committee, and reports can be made anonymously. Proven cases lead to suspension or expulsion.

Academic Integrity
Plagiarism and cheating are serious offences. A first offence results in a zero on the assignment and a written warning. A second offence results in an F grade in the course and referral to the disciplinary committee.

Grievance Redressal
A student may submit a written grievance to the Student Grievance Cell. The cell replies within 15 working days. A student who is not satisfied may appeal to the Dean of Students.

Library Rules
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. The fine for late return is Rs 2 per day per book. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current price.

Official Notices
Official notices are published on the notice portal. Students are responsible for checking the portal at least once a week.
