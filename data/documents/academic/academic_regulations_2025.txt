title: Academic Regulations 2025
category: academic
audience: student
effective: 2025-07-01

Course Registration
Students must complete course registration within the first 7 days of each semester. Late registration is allowed until day 14 with a late fee of Rs 500 and written approval from the Dean of Academic Affairs. Registration after day 14 is not permitted.

Course Withdrawal
A student may withdraw from a course until the end of week 9 of the semester. Withdrawal requires approval from the course instructor and the faculty advisor. A withdrawn course appears on the transcript with the grade W and does not count toward the CGPA. A student must keep at least 12 credits in the semester after withdrawal.

Attendance Requirement
Students must attend at least 75% of the classes in every course. A student with attendance below 75% may be barred from the end-semester examination. The Dean of Academic Affairs may condone a shortfall of up to 10% on production of a medical certificate.

Grading and Academic Probation
Grades range from AA (10 points) to FF (0 points, fail). The CGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points across all completed courses. A student whose CGPA falls below 5.0 for two consecutive semesters is placed on academic probation.

Re-evaluation of Answer Scripts
A student may request re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within 5 working days of the grade release. The fee is Rs 300 per course. The re-evaluation grade replaces the original grade only if it is higher.
